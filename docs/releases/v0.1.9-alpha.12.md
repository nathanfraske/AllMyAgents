# AllMyAgents v0.1.9-alpha.12

Released 2026-07-31.

## Journal recovery reliability

- Fixed a race in exclusive recovery-metadata publication. An exact concurrent reader can finish
  removing the publisher's hard-link partial after validating the durable final without making the
  original publisher fail or take the journal offline.
- The publisher accepts that concurrent cleanup only after revalidating the surviving final as a regular,
  non-symlink file on the expected volume, with one link and byte-for-byte identical content. Missing,
  replaced, multiply linked, or changed targets still fail closed.
- Preserved strict no-replace publication semantics: a competing or ambiguous target is never overwritten.
- Added a regression that forces a reader to complete root-binding publication at the exact hard-link
  boundary and proves enrollment resumes with the intended root and journal identity.
- Bounded the repeated Windows directory-durability stress test to 15 seconds so CI cannot hang while
  still proving barrier files remain fixed-size across successive publications.

## Scope

This cut contains only the recovery publication fix and its tests. It does not change the journal schema,
discard journal content, or broaden automatic recovery eligibility.
